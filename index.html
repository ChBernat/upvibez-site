Scheduling

Build a schedule down to the minute, for as many months ahead as you want.

Set start and end times for each block and assign what plays: a playlist, a full album, or a single track scheduled on its own. Repeat a block across days or carve out an exception for a holiday. Every venue in the group schedules independently, and every room within a venue does too, so the lobby can run a lounge playlist through breakfast while the restaurant runs something else. A venue that has not split into rooms yet gets one schedule, the venue default, and can add rooms whenever it is ready.

Playback

Play the schedule on any device, in any room, across every venue in the group.

Install the player on as many desktop and tablet devices as a venue needs, one per room or more where a room needs backup coverage, with output routed through the venue's own volume control into the house sound system. Each device keeps its own room's schedule and caches a week of music, so playback stays right if the connection drops and syncs the moment it is back. Every venue and every room reports the same way, so an operations lead can check any of them from one place.

Compliance

Every track is licensed, and every play is logged, room by room.

Music entering a schedule is cleared for commercial use before it plays. Playback is logged automatically as it happens, so a venue's play history can be produced on demand: the room, the time, and confirmation that the track was licensed. That record is what a licensing partner needs to verify use, and what an operations team needs to show a rights holder, for any venue in the group.
